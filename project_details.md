Here is a comprehensive project blueprint for the **Hotel Association of Nepal Sudurpashchim (HANS)** website. This document covers the strategic vision, UI/UX concepts, page architecture, and functional requirements without using any code.

---

 1. Project Overview & Strategic Vision
**Project Name:** Hotel Association of Nepal Sudurpashchim (HANS)
**Context:** This provincial chapter focuses specifically on the 8 districts of Sudurpashchim Province (Kailali, Kanchanpur, Doti, Bajhang, Bajura, Achham, Dadeldhura, Baitadi). 
**Core Objectives:**
*   Unite and empower hospitality stakeholders in the province.
*   Promote Sudurpashchim as a premier, off-the-beaten-path tourism destination (highlighting areas like Khaptad, Shuklaphanta, Api Nampa, and Dodhara Chandani).
*   Provide a centralized, searchable directory of member hotels, resorts, and homestays.

---

 2. UI/UX Concept & Design System
The design should reflect the natural beauty, tranquility, and emerging luxury of Sudurpashchim, while maintaining the professional authority of a national association chapter.

**Color Palette:**
*   **Primary Color (Green):** Represents the lush forests of Shuklaphanta, the hills of Khaptad, and eco-tourism. Use a deep, rich "Forest Green" for primary buttons, headers, and active states, paired with a soft "Mint/Sage Green" for backgrounds and accents.
*   **Secondary Color (Blue):** Represents the clear skies of the Himalayas, the Mahakali River, and trust/professionalism. Use a "Deep River Blue" for text, links, and secondary accents, with a "Light Sky Blue" for hover states and informational banners.
*   **Neutrals:** Crisp whites, soft off-whites, and slate grays for backgrounds and text to ensure high readability.

**Typography:**
*   **Headings:** A modern, elegant Serif font (e.g., *Playfair Display* or *Merriweather*) to give a premium, hospitable feel.
*   **Body Text:** A clean, highly legible Sans-Serif font (e.g., *Inter*, *Poppins*, or *Roboto*) for easy reading on all devices.

**Imagery & Visuals:**
*   Avoid generic stock photos. Use high-quality, authentic photography of Sudurpashchim’s landscapes, cultural festivals, local food, and actual member hotels.
*   Use subtle wave or mountain-line dividers to break up sections, nodding to the province's geography.

**UX Principles:**
*   **Mobile-First:** The majority of local users will access the site via smartphones. The UI must be thumb-friendly.
*   **Accessibility:** High contrast ratios, scalable text, and clear focus states for keyboard navigation.

---

3. Information Architecture (Page Concepts)

 A. Home Page
*   **Hero Section:** A full-width, high-quality video background or image slider showcasing the province's beauty and hospitality. Overlay text: "Discover the Heart of Hospitality in Sudurpashchim."
*   **Quick Stats:** Animated counters showing Total Members, Districts Covered, Rooms Available, and Years of Service.
*   **Featured Destinations/Hotels:** A carousel of top-rated member hotels or specific tourism zones (e.g., "Stay in Kailali", "Explore Doti").
*   **Call to Action (CTA):** "Become a Member" and "Explore Member Hotels".

B. About Us
Introduction: History of HAN Sudurpashchim, Vision, Mission, and Core Values.
Executive Committee: Grid profiles of the current President, Vice Presidents, Secretary, and members, including their photos, designations, and hotel affiliations.
Structure & Affiliation: How the provincial chapter connects with HAN Central and the Ministry of Culture, Tourism, and Civil Aviation.
Downloads: Access to the association's constitution, bylaws, and annual reports.

C. Membership
Why Join: Benefits of membership (networking, training, government advocacy, promotional benefits).
Categories: Definitions of membership types (Corporate, Institutional, Life, Associate).
How to Apply: Step-by-step guide with downloadable application forms.
Member Login/Portal: A secure login area for existing members to update their hotel details, view invoices, and pay annual subscription fees.

D. Hotel Directory (The Core Feature)
Interactive Map: A custom map of Sudurpashchim Province. Clicking on a district (e.g., Kanchanpur) highlights hotels in that area.
Advanced Search & Filters: Filter by District, Hotel Category (Star-rated, Boutique, Resort, Homestay, Budget), Amenities (Wi-Fi, Parking, Pool), and Price Range.
Hotel Profile Pages: Individual pages for each member hotel featuring:
Photo gallery and video tour.
Detailed description and unique selling propositions.
Room types and facilities.
Contact info, physical address, and embedded map.
Direct "Book Now" using whatsapp (linking to the hotel's own website or a standardized inquiry form).

E. Contact Us
Inquiry Form: For general queries, complaints, or partnership proposals.
Office Details: Physical address of the HAN Sudurpashchim secretariat, phone numbers, and official email.
Interactive Map: Embedded map showing the exact location of the association's office.
Social Media Links: Icons linking to Facebook, LinkedIn, etc.
